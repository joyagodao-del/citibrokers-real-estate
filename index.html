Home Buyers

Find the right home with a clear plan.

Personalized Search

Aydee helps buyers define priorities, compare neighborhoods, understand pricing, and focus on homes that match their lifestyle and long-term goals.

The process starts with a conversation about budget, location, must-have features, timeline, commute needs, school preferences, and future plans. From there, Aydee helps narrow the search so buyers spend less time guessing and more time viewing homes that genuinely fit.

Offer Strategy

From market context to terms and timing, buyers receive practical guidance before writing an offer so they can compete with confidence.

Aydee helps review comparable sales, seller expectations, offer strength, inspection timelines, financing details, and closing flexibility. Her goal is to help buyers make smart decisions without feeling rushed or overwhelmed.

Tour Guidance

Every showing is an opportunity to look beyond the surface. Aydee helps buyers notice layout, condition, resale potential, neighborhood fit, and possible concerns before moving forward.

Buyers receive steady guidance on what questions to ask, what details to compare, and how to decide whether a property is worth pursuing.

Closing Support

After an offer is accepted, Aydee helps clients stay organized through inspections, appraisal, lender communication, deadlines, final walkthrough, and closing day.

The goal is to keep each step clear, reduce surprises, and help buyers feel prepared when it is time to receive the keys.

Home Sellers

Position your home to stand out.

Pricing & Preparation

Sellers receive thoughtful pricing guidance, preparation recommendations, and a launch plan designed to create strong first impressions.

Aydee reviews comparable homes, active competition, recent sales, condition, upgrades, neighborhood trends, and buyer expectations. She helps sellers understand how pricing affects attention, showings, negotiation strength, and time on market.

Marketing & Negotiation

Citibrokers presents each property with polished visuals, clear listing copy, and negotiation support focused on protecting the seller's goals.

A strong listing needs more than a sign in the yard. Aydee helps coordinate the property story, photography, online presentation, showing readiness, offer review, and communication so sellers can move through the sale with confidence.

Listing Launch Plan

Before a home goes live, Aydee helps sellers prepare the details that shape buyer perception, including cleaning, staging suggestions, curb appeal, repairs, and timing.

The launch plan is built to make the home feel intentional, professional, and easy for qualified buyers to understand quickly.

Offer Review & Closing

When offers arrive, Aydee helps sellers compare more than price. She reviews financing, contingencies, timelines, closing costs, buyer strength, and risk.

From contract to closing, sellers receive organized communication and guidance through inspections, appraisal, negotiations, and final settlement.

FAQ

Common real estate questions.

When should I speak with a real estate agent?

Early is best. A consultation can help you understand timing, preparation, pricing, financing, and next steps before pressure builds.

Do I need pre-approval before touring homes?

Pre-approval helps buyers understand budget, move faster, and submit stronger offers when the right home appears.

How do sellers prepare for listing?

Preparation usually includes pricing review, repairs or touch-ups, cleaning, staging guidance, photography, and a launch strategy.

Can Aydee help with luxury or investment homes?

Yes. Citibrokers Real Estate supports buyers and sellers across traditional homes, luxury properties, and investment opportunities.
